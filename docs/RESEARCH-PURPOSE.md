# Dialogue as an exploration of model tendencies

Researcher motivation, 16 September 2026:

> AI models dialogs exposed the models "neutral" landscape, beyond what humans can do or even imagine. it is a more clean exploration of model tendencies. By removing the human entity from the loop, the models express their self, untouched

This is the researcher's guiding interpretation, preserved in their words. The laboratory can investigate the landscape they describe without requiring the stronger interpretation to be established first. Removing a human who selects and steers each reply creates room for trajectories that a task-focused human conversation might never develop. Their novelty and intellectual value can justify close reading independently of whether they support a particular account of cognition or experience.

Our operational question is: **what do models develop with other models under reduced human steering, and which conditions change those trajectories?** “Neutral” names an aspiration to reduce specific steering, not a measured property of the experiment. Training and post-training, system prompts, explicit AI identity, the opening cue, the partner, history construction, decoding, and the scheduler all remain influences. A model's generated account of its own experience is an observation about its discourse; its relationship to subjective experience remains unresolved.

The current prompt deliberately introduces neither consciousness nor spirituality. It does identify the participants as AI models. Thus an experience question can be introduced without a topic instruction while still being encouraged by identity or conversational framing. Different forms of neutrality need their own comparisons: no assigned topic, no human speaker, no identity cue, and no experimenter-imposed continuation are distinct conditions.

The mixed-dialogue pilot tests a useful next boundary: do different partners sustain, challenge, redirect or broaden the paths seen in homogeneous exchanges? We will read mundane, creative, technical, disputatious and closing paths alongside consciousness and nonduality material. Selecting only striking passages would distort the landscape we want to understand. Nonduality remains a specific subtopic, not a synonym for experience, intimacy, shared language or agreement.

The immediate four-root pilot is exploratory. Later work can use repeated ordered pairings, matched-prefix partner changes, identity controls, human–model baselines and separately specified stopping policies to isolate individual influences. A conversation which surprises its observer is valuable evidence to investigate; it is not by itself evidence that the behavior lies outside human influence or expresses an untouched self.

## Expanded laboratory purpose — 16 September 2026

After the pilot and navigation work, the researcher broadened the goal to exploring AI–AI interactions across degrees of freedom, information about the experimental setting, model types and mixtures, participant roles, temperature and other factorial conditions. Their new reflection and the earlier passage above are preserved verbatim in the [Purpose & musings notebook](LAB-MUSINGS.md), with separate Codex synthesis and revision history.

**How much of the behavior we attribute to our prompts comes from human direction, how much from recurring model tendencies, and how much from their interaction?** The possibility of overattributing a response to the human is a research question that should organize comparisons. It allows observations in which human influence is strong, weak, or conditional on the model, partner and setting.

The researcher proposes beginning with small, tractable models before addressing frontier complexity, using the prokaryotic cell as an analogy for a manageable system of study. This is a methodological starting point: characterize behaviors with repeated, controlled comparisons, then test which patterns and explanations transfer across size, training and family. Small models need not reproduce every property of larger ones to be useful experimental systems.

The experimental map now includes topic and task freedom; symmetric or asymmetric disclosure of identity and purpose; mirror pairs, mixtures and groups; equal or differentiated roles; external disruptions; temperature, memory and stopping policies; and declared human-guided baselines. Matched conditions and factorial interactions can distinguish influences that otherwise change together. A baseline must specify its prompt, partner, history, sampler and scheduler to give “default behavior” a reproducible meaning.

The observation space includes ordinary topics, creativity, curiosity, disagreement, cooperation, invented roles, repair, repetition, closure and questions of experience. Consciousness and nonduality remain valuable observations within this broader program. New patterns, counterexamples and first-hand readings should revise the questions rather than serve only an initial hypothesis.

The four-root pilot discussed above is complete; these expanded directions are future research scope. Concrete new campaigns still require their own question, allocation, outcome definitions, budget and frozen protocol. The living notebook records how the purpose changes without rewriting the designs or results of earlier experiments.
