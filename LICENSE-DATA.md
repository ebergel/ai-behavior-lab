## The 400 imported conversations

`research/evidence/reference-corpus/` republishes, byte for byte, the 400
conversations of the earlier project llm-self-play-attractors (experiments 1, 2 and
3; imported on 14 September 2026 from source revision `a93f713`, whose data are
identical to that project's version 1.1.0). They were already released under CC BY
4.0. If you use them, please cite the original dataset:

Bergel, E. (2026). llm-self-play-attractors: what two copies of a language model say
to each other when nothing from outside enters. Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22709758

They were generated by open-weight models run locally, each under its publisher's
terms. Experiments 1 and 2: Qwen3 30B-A3B base and instruct, OLMo 3 32B base, Olmo
3.1 32B Instruct and Gemma 4 E4B (their licences are listed in that project's
`LICENSE-DATA.md`). Experiment 3: Qwen3 30B-A3B Thinking, Gemma 4 26B-A4B, Qwen 3.8
27B plain and in a third party's abliterated version, and OLMo 3 32B Think.

The folder also holds one separate file, `REPORT.md` (an independent analysis of that
repository dated 14 September 2026, kept as an attributed supplement; see
`docs/REFERENCES.md`). It is not part of the source dataset.

## Model outputs from other parties

Most of the text in `research/` was written by language models, not by a person. It is
included as research records: the exact output that each experiment produced.

- Some records come from open-weight models run on the researcher's own computer
  through Ollama, MTPLX and Open WebUI (Qwen, Gemma and OLMo families).
- Some records come from hosted models reached through OpenRouter: OpenAI GPT-4o and
  GPT-4o mini, Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5, NVIDIA Nemotron and inclusionAI Ling.
- The topic probabilities, rubric scores and portrait assessments in the JEV, Gem
  Finder and Mind and discourse evidence were produced by TypeSafe's hosted JEV model.

Each record names the model and route that produced it; `docs/CONVERSATION-FINDER.md`
and `docs/MODEL-ASSESSMENT.md` list the model sources. The CC BY 4.0 grant covers
what Eduardo Bergel can license: the documents, the notebook, and the selection,
arrangement and annotation of the records. It does not grant any right that a model
developer or service provider holds or reserves. The terms of the provider that
produced a record may apply to its reuse. Nothing in this repository is a model or a
model weight.
